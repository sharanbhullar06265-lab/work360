## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Native Execution of Original Section Components & Styles**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Run the exact section component implementations and embedded CSS rules with a lightweight compatibility shim and centralized CMS data bridge.
  - *Why*: Guarantees pixel-for-pixel fidelity on every custom canvas animation, SVG radar, typography scale, and responsive breakpoint shown in your 47 screenshots.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Hand-approximating the sections with generic Tailwind cards was rejected because it alters spacing, misses subtle interactive physics, and caused the visual discrepancies you flagged earlier.

- **Decision 2: Centralized Content & Brand Bridge (`SITE` + `CMS` Data Store)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Drive all 12 sections from a single structured data bridge (containing the agency name, contact details, 6 services, 6 case studies, 6 team members, 6 reviews, 3 pricing plans, 8 FAQs, and 6 insights posts) plus smooth in-page anchor navigation (`#services`, `#work`, `#pricing`, `#about`, `#blog`, `#contact`).
  - *Why*: Lets you inspect the exact original **Reachwise** site first and then change the brand name, colours, or text in one place whenever you are ready.

- **Interactive Component & State Mapping**:
  - **Hero Spotlight & Counter (`RwHero`)**: Tracks pointer coordinates over the aerial crowd canvas to render the angled lime beam and click-to-launch campaign ripples while incrementing the monthly reach counter.
  - **Radar & Crowd Simulations (`RwClients`, `RwIntro`)**: Runs `requestAnimationFrame` loops for the sweeping degree radar (updating `LAST PING` client metrics) and the 1,200-dot crowd canvas (clustering buyers into Search, Feeds, and Audiences).
  - **Horizontal Services Accordion (`RwServices`)**: Manages active index `0..5`, expanding the selected channel card to reveal its custom interactive graphic while collapsing the other 5 into vertical rotated tabs.
  - **Before/After Case Slider (`RwWork`)**: Tracks active client tab `0..5` and pointer drag position `0..100%` to clip the grayscale `BEFORE` layer against the full-color `AFTER` layer and animate the SVG growth curve.
  - **Trapezoid Funnel & Lime Report (`RwFunnel`, `RwNumbers`)**: Syncs active step `0..3` to fill trapezoid stages with animated falling particles; switches KPI datasets and SVG sparklines across `OVERVIEW`, `SEO`, `SOCIAL`, and `ADS` tabs.
  - **Team Radar, iPhone Reviews, Pricing, FAQ & Footer (`RwTeam`, `RwReviews`, `RwPricing`, `RwFaq`, `RwCta`, `RwFooter`)**: Tracks cursor position across team cards (`SPECIALISTS REACHED`) and the giant footer wordmark, cycles iOS lockscreen review notifications, toggles `MONTHLY` vs `QUARTERLY -10%` pricing, expands FAQ items along the vertical lime progress bar, and validates the `Ready to be seen?` audit form.
